### in my laptop, on my laptop
install something on my laptop.（可以理解为在电脑操作系统“之上”，所以不是用in，而是用on）



### "compared to" 实际上是一个“介词”
"What is new in JDK 17 compared to JDK 8?" （compared to是一个介词，或者可以认为是一个固定搭配。所以这里不是comparing to，或者compared with）



### "On the bus"
对于交通工具类，一般认为可以在里面走动的具有比较大空间的，使用on：on a train, on a ship, on the space shuttle, on a passenger plane
较小空间无法走动的，使用in：in a car, in a small boat, in a helicopter, in a rocket, in a hot air balloon, in a small plane (think of an old biplane). 
对于没有“围墙”的，也用on：on a skateboard, on ice skates (platforms for your feet). On a horse, on a bike



### "On the client", "In the client"
当指软件或进程运作在客户端时，需要用"on the client"，这一点与"on the laptop"类似。



### in the position, at the position
In this position 一般指职位、责任：
"In this position, you will be responsible for managing a team of ten people."
"In this position, the player has a better chance of scoring a goal."

At this position 一般指位置（某个元素位于序列中的位置、某个步骤位于流程中的位置）：
"The molecule binds at this position on the DNA strand."
"The guard stands at this position outside the door."
